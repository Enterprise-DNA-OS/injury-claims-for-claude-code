# Why this rebuild

Chosen 2 November 2026 as the first rebuild in the injury and workers' compensation claims family.

- **The bill.** Solv publishes no price. Its pricing page says customers pay a one-time setup fee and an annual licence fee, quoted on the size of the business, its requirements and its injury and claim volumes (https://solv.com.au/pricing/, checked 2 November 2026). The same page says it is used by 70% of self-insurance licences, 18% of Comcare licences and 32% of the ASX 100. Its closest rival, Figtree Workers Compensation Claims, is listed on software directories from A$35,000 (not a price the vendor publishes).
- **Data-shaped.** Sites, workers, injuries, claims, certificates of capacity, return to work plans, payments, reimbursements, actions and case notes, and weekly rituals: telling the insurer, forwarding claims, chasing certificates, reviewing plans, chasing reimbursements.
- **The rules are real and checkable.** NSW 1998 Act ss 44, 63, 261 and 267 and 1987 Act ss 36 and 37; Victoria's WIRC Act 2013 s 73(1), s 75 and the mental injury notice; Queensland's WCRA 2003 s 133(3); New Zealand's Accident Compensation Act 2001 ss 56 to 58, 97 and 98; WHS Act s 38 and HSWA s 56. They turn into dates, rates and refusals.
- **What stays with the custom build.** The insurer feed (icare's interface), regulator submission files for self-insurers, a worker portal for lodging injuries from a phone, and the schedules for WA, SA, Tasmania, the territories and Comcare.
